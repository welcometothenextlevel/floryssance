# Floryssance

One-page site for Floryssance (@floryssance_chay), handmade artificial-rose bouquets in Chavannes-près-Renens. Plain static HTML/CSS/JS, French, served by GitHub Pages from the repo root.

## Files
- `index.html`: the page
- `assets/site.css`, `assets/site.js`: styles, the bouquet builder and the photo viewer
- `assets/img/`: all 13 photos from the 5 Instagram posts, at 600 and 1100 px, as JPG and WebP
- `_build/images.py`: makes `assets/img/` from the raw downloads in `_src/ig/` (gitignored)

## Where the facts come from
All from the Instagram profile and post captions (November 2026):
- name, Chavannes-près-Renens, artificial flowers, orders by Instagram DM
- bouquets of 10, 20 and 25 roses, initials, rhinestone crown, lilies, cards ("Merci", "Les plus belles fleurs pour la plus belle femme")

## Not confirmed yet
- **Prices, wrapping and payment details.** These are in the Instagram story highlights (Tarifs, Infos/emballage, Paiement), which need a login. The site points to those highlights and shows no prices.
- **Handover or delivery.** The site says the handover is arranged by message.
- The "Retours" highlight (probably customer feedback) could become a testimonials section.
- The TikTok in the bio (@florys_chay) no longer exists, so it isn't linked.
- The three post videos aren't used (only photos were asked for).

`noindex` is on while this is a pitch. Remove it from `index.html` once the client approves.
